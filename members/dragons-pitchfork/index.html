I came in through a glass kitchen table in Digital Kwan's first apartment, pushing pens in front of a boombox full of beat tapes. Kwan carried those tapes to Father Lord, and that got me into what was still called the Shaolin Clan. Lord and King Cee had seniority, so I fell back and watched. At The Temple, Lord taught martial arts, philosophy and science mostly by example. In all those sessions he gave me exactly one explicit tip on a beat. Everything else I learned by watching him work. I made Zen Master for him, and Wen Tzu for him and Buddha Love. As short aXe I rhymed on 20 Bags of Gold and Super Natural. After Lord passed in 1997 I walked away, and short aXe stopped fitting. It took years and more than one name, YukoN BLaCK among them, to find my way back. Dragon's Pitchfork is who came out the other side.

"Mental planes fly to Venus on the White Owl's wings"short aXe on Super Natural, 1997

These days I build out loud. In 2021 I co-founded GRYSKLL, a Hip Hop & Creative Wellness collective, and I run House of Fluid Reflections with my wife, Yani. I reconnected with my brothers in 2024. In 2025 I performed in the Super Natural video and recorded KraftWorks with Digital Kwan and B. Ooh. Now I'm filming Killa Bee Movie, the story of The Killa Bee That Couldn't, and helping bring what Lord left in the vault into the light. The equipment is still here. So is the music. I don't intend to let it die on a hard drive.
