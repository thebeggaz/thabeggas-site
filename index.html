01 / The Temple

Kung fu on the VCR. Beats on the ASR.

The Temple was where it happened. Kung fu movies played while the music got made, and the samples came straight off VHS. Super Natural came out of that room in 1997.

Father Lord passed that same year, months after the first single. The collective he founded kept moving as one mind.
